Privacy Policy - Forgotten Front Poster

Effective date: June 20, 2026

Forgotten Front Poster ("the App") is a private tool used by the operator of the Forgotten Front channel to upload their own video content to their own TikTok account. This policy explains what the App accesses and how that information is handled.

Who this applies to

The App is used solely by the Forgotten Front channel operator. It does not have other users, and it does not collect, process, or store information about anyone else.

What the App accesses

When you authorize the App through TikTok Login, TikTok provides the App with an access token tied to your own TikTok account, limited to the video.upload permission. The App uses this only to upload finished video files to your TikTok account as private drafts.

The App does not read your TikTok content, followers, messages, analytics, or any other account data. It cannot post publicly on your behalf - uploaded videos remain as drafts until you manually review and publish them in the TikTok app.

What the App stores

  • OAuth tokens (access token and refresh token) are stored locally on the operator's own computer, in a private, access-restricted file, so the App does not need to re-authenticate on every run. They are never transmitted to any third party.
  • Video files and captions that the operator chooses to upload are sent directly to TikTok's official Content Posting API and to no one else.

What the App does NOT do

  • It does not sell, share, or disclose any data to third parties.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • It does not collect personal information from any other person.

Data retention and deletion

OAuth tokens live only in the local credentials file and can be deleted at any time by removing that file or revoking the App's access from your TikTok account settings, which immediately and permanently ends the App's access.
